Fair Questions

Before you ask

Does it cost anything to join? +

Nope — signing up is completely free, and there's no cover charge from us, ever. You just pay for your own drinks at the bar like any other night out. Our money comes from deals with the bars, not from fans.

How will I hear about my night? +

By text message — that's why we ask for your mobile number. When one of your bands hits 100 fans near your ZIP and a takeover is locked in, you'll get a text with the bar's name and location, the date, and the start time. No app to check, no feed to scroll.

What if I can't make it that night? +

Nothing happens. There's no ticket, no RSVP, and no obligation — the text is an invitation, not a commitment. Miss one and you'll still get texted for the next takeover for your bands. Life happens; the mob understands.

What do you do with my phone number? +

One thing only: texting you about Bar Flash Mob takeovers for your bands near your ZIP. Your number is never sold and never shared with anyone — not bars, not advertisers, not anybody. Reply STOP to any text and you're out, no hard feelings.

My favorite band is pretty niche. Is there any point? +

Yes — honestly, niche is where this gets fun. Every band counts, and meters fill band-by-band, ZIP-by-ZIP. You might be fan #4 for your band today, but you'd be amazed how many people within 20 minutes of you have the same "nobody around here likes them" band on their list.
